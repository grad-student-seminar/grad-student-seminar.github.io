Tuesday October 6th
Colby Shaw
Laminations, Foliations, and Train Tracks: A Crash Course
We will give an introduction to the connections between laminations, foliations, and train tracks on surfaces. We will then briefly discuss how these tools appear in Thurston’s classification of orientation-preserving surface homeomorphisms up to isotopy, and mention some of the many other areas of mathematics in which they arise.

Denali Relles
Stable Homotopy Theory: Topology and Algebra
Topological spaces are great, but do you ever wish that they were more like abelian groups? I know I do! That's why I study spectra. A spectrum (for me) is an object which can be a topological space that has been "stabilized", but in another sense is an abelian group "up to homotopy". In this talk, I will explain what this means, and why it is useful.
